Abstract

This document presents the sensitivity analysis conducted for the Quantum-Veil Sovereignty Security Index (QSSI) 2026 Definitive World Edition.

The objective of the analysis is to evaluate the stability of sovereign rankings under controlled perturbations applied to composite scores generated by the definitive QVP GLOBAL SYSTEM™ computational pipeline.

Sensitivity analysis provides empirical evidence regarding ranking resilience, robustness of comparative outcomes, and the extent to which minor variations in observed values may influence final sovereign capability assessments.

All reported results originate directly from execution of the archived computational workflow and reproducible analytical environment.

---

1. Purpose

Sensitivity analysis was conducted to evaluate:

- Ranking stability
- Numerical robustness
- Perturbation resilience
- Composite score consistency
- Comparative ordering preservation
- Decision-support reliability

The analysis examines whether modest disturbances in underlying values materially alter sovereign rankings.

3. Sensitivity Testing Framework

Controlled stochastic perturbations were introduced to QSSI composite scores.

The procedure evaluates the degree to which ranking structures remain stable under progressively increasing levels of simulated uncertainty.

For each perturbation scenario:

1. Random noise was added to QSSI scores.
2. Rankings were recomputed.
3. Rank-order agreement was measured using Spearman rank correlation.

6. Interpretation

The results indicate that modest score perturbations did not materially alter the sovereign ranking structure generated by the QSSI framework.

This finding supports the stability of comparative outcomes under the evaluated uncertainty ranges.

8. Methodological Considerations

Sensitivity analysis evaluates ranking behavior under simulated perturbations rather than real-world structural changes.

Accordingly:

- Results should be interpreted within the tested noise ranges.
- Stability does not imply immunity to major dataset revisions.
- Future editions may exhibit different sensitivity characteristics depending on data composition and coverage.

The analysis therefore provides empirical evidence of observed stability rather than a guarantee of invariance under all possible conditions.
