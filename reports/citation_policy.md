1. Purpose

This Citation Policy establishes the official requirements for citing, referencing, reproducing, adapting, or discussing any component of the FAIR+D Canon™ Strategic Capability Framework.

The policy applies to:

- SCI (Strategic Capability Index)
- SCI+
- SCI ULTRA
- FAIR+D Canon™ methodology
- Rankings
- Datasets
- Statistical outputs
- Validation reports
- Figures and tables
- Supplementary materials
- Derived analytical works

Proper citation ensures transparency, reproducibility, academic integrity, and accurate attribution.

---

2. Recommended Primary Citation

All scholarly, professional, institutional, governmental, and public references should cite the current edition as follows:

Mazumdar, B. (2026).

FAIR+D Canon™ Strategic Capability Framework: SCI, SCI+, and SCI ULTRA (2026 Edition).

Zenodo.

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20385492

FAIR+D Canon™ Strategic Capability Framework.

8. Attribution Requirements

Any reuse of framework outputs should include:

- Author name
- Framework title
- Edition year
- DOI reference

Minimum attribution:

"Source: FAIR+D Canon™ Strategic Capability Framework (Mazumdar, 2026)."

---

9. Derivative Works

Researchers may create:

- Replications
- Extensions
- Comparative analyses
- Benchmarking studies
- Methodological evaluations

Derivative works should clearly indicate:

- Original FAIR+D Canon™ source
- Modifications performed
- Additional data sources incorporated

Derivative works must not be represented as official FAIR+D Canon™ releases unless explicitly authorized.
