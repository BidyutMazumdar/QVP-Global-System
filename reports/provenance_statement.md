Provenance Statement

Data Lineage and Framework Provenance

Purpose

This document records the origin, transformation, integration, and publication history of the FAIR+D Canon™ framework and associated datasets.

Source Datasets

The framework incorporates publicly available information from:

- OECD AI Policy Observatory
- Oxford Insights Government AI Readiness Index
- World Governance Indicators (WGI)
- National Cyber Security Index (NCSI)
- ND-GAIN
- IMF-related resilience indicators

Ownership of original datasets remains with their respective institutions.

Data Processing

The FAIR+D Canon™ framework performs:

- harmonization
- normalization
- score transformation
- composite construction
- confidence adjustment
- validation testing

to generate new analytical outputs.

Derived Outputs

The following datasets are original FAIR+D Canon™ outputs:

- SCI_2026_v1_MC_Canon
- SCI_PLUS_2026_v1_MC_Canon
- SCI_ULTRA_2026_v1_Fair+DCanon
- AI_INDEX_2026_v1_MC_Canon
- LEGAL_WGI_2026_v1_MC_Canon
- RES_INDEX_2026_MC_Canon
- PQC_NCSI_2026_MC_Canon
