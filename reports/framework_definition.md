Framework Definition

FAIR+D Canon™ Strategic Capability Framework

Purpose

FAIR+D Canon™ is a multidimensional analytical framework designed to evaluate national strategic capability through the integration of technological readiness, institutional quality, resilience capacity, policy preparedness, and evidence confidence.

The framework was developed to address a persistent limitation in international benchmarking systems: the tendency to evaluate countries through isolated indicators rather than through an integrated capability architecture.

Core Principle

The framework is based on the proposition that sustainable national capability emerges from the interaction of:

- Technological Capacity
- Governance Quality
- Systemic Resilience
- Policy Preparedness
- Evidence Reliability

No single dimension is sufficient to explain long-term strategic performance.

Framework Architecture

SCI (Strategic Capability Index)

SCI measures the interaction between:

- AI Readiness
- Governance Effectiveness

SCI represents the foundational capability layer.

SCI+

SCI extends SCI by incorporating:

- Policy Quality Capacity (PQC)

SCI+ captures institutional preparedness and implementation capacity.

SCI ULTRA

SCI ULTRA expands the framework through:

- Strategic Capability
- Resilience Capacity
- Confidence Adjustment

SCI ULTRA represents the highest-order capability assessment within the FAIR+D Canon™ framework.

Analytical Objectives

The framework supports:

- International benchmarking
- Governance assessment
- Strategic capability analysis
- Resilience evaluation
- Policy prioritization
- Comparative research
- Evidence-based decision support

Conceptual Contribution

FAIR+D Canon™ shifts evaluation from isolated performance metrics toward an integrated capability perspective that recognizes the interdependence of technology, governance, resilience, and institutional quality.
