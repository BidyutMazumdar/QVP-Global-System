Executive Summary

FAIR+D Canon™ Strategic Capability Framework (2026 Edition)

Overview

The FAIR+D Canon™ Strategic Capability Framework is a multidimensional analytical system designed to evaluate national capability across technology, governance, resilience, preparedness, and evidence confidence dimensions.

The framework was developed to address the growing need for integrated capability assessment in an era characterized by rapid technological transformation, geopolitical uncertainty, climate-related risks, and increasing demands for institutional adaptability.

Rather than relying on a single indicator, the FAIR+D Canon™ framework combines multiple complementary dimensions into a coherent measurement architecture that captures both present capability and future readiness.

The 2026 edition introduces three interconnected indices:

- Strategic Capability Index (SCI 2026)
- Strategic Capability Plus (SCI+ 2026)
- Strategic Capability Ultra (SCI_ULTRA 2026)

Together, these measures provide a comprehensive evidence-based approach for evaluating national strategic performance and long-term development potential.

---

Framework Architecture

The FAIR+D Canon™ system integrates four principal dimensions:

Artificial Intelligence Capability

Measured through a composite AI Index constructed from internationally recognized indicators of technological readiness and AI ecosystem development.

Governance Quality

Measured through institutional indicators including:

- Rule of Law
- Regulatory Quality
- Government Effectiveness
- Control of Corruption

Resilience Capacity

Measured through national resilience indicators capturing adaptive capacity and long-term sustainability.

Preparedness Quality

Measured through forward-looking preparedness metrics reflecting readiness for future technological and societal challenges.

SCI_ULTRA Innovation

A major contribution of the FAIR+D Canon™ framework is the introduction of confidence-adjusted capability measurement.

SCI_ULTRA incorporates:

- Capability assessment
- Evidence confidence
- Ranking adjustment
- Transparency mechanisms

This approach allows users to distinguish between observed performance and confidence in the underlying evidence.

The framework therefore advances beyond traditional ranking systems that treat all observations as equally reliable.

Conclusion

The FAIR+D Canon™ Strategic Capability Framework provides a statistically robust, theoretically grounded, and policy-relevant system for evaluating national capability in the twenty-first century.

By integrating technological readiness, governance quality, resilience capacity, preparedness, and evidence confidence into a unified analytical architecture, the framework offers a comprehensive perspective on national strategic performance.

The empirical results indicate that long-term capability is shaped not by technology alone, but by the interaction of institutions, resilience, preparedness, and innovation capacity.

Consequently, the FAIR+D Canon™ framework offers a valuable foundation for comparative international research, strategic planning, policy evaluation, and evidence-based decision support.
