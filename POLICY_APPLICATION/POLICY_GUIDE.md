Purpose

This document provides guidance for the interpretation and policy application of QSSI 2026 results.

The objective is to support:

- governments
- policymakers
- national security institutions
- digital governance agencies
- cybersecurity authorities
- international organizations
- academic researchers

in the responsible use of QSSI outputs.

---

What QSSI Measures

QSSI evaluates national capability across four strategic dimensions:

1. Artificial Intelligence Capacity

2. Legal and Governance Quality

3. National Resilience

4. Post-Quantum Cybersecurity Readiness

These dimensions collectively represent sovereign preparedness for the emerging AI and quantum era.

---

Intended Uses

QSSI may be used for:

- comparative benchmarking
- strategic planning
- technology governance assessment
- cybersecurity preparedness evaluation
- resilience analysis
- national capability monitoring
- policy prioritization
- international comparison

---

Non-Intended Uses

QSSI should not be used as:

- a geopolitical forecasting model
- a military power index
- a national competitiveness ranking
- a measure of regime legitimacy
- a prediction of future conflict
- a substitute for expert policy assessment

QSSI is a comparative analytical framework.

---

Interpreting QSSI Scores

Higher scores indicate stronger integrated capability across:

- AI readiness
- governance quality
- resilience
- post-quantum preparedness

Lower scores indicate greater strategic capacity constraints.

Scores should always be interpreted relative to the international distribution.

---

Interpreting Rankings

Rankings provide comparative positioning among participating countries.

A higher rank does not imply superiority across all dimensions.

Countries may achieve similar scores through different capability combinations.

Users should examine:

- indicator values
- contribution analysis
- regional context
- tier classification

alongside rank positions.

Limitations for Policymakers

Users should recognize:

1. Coverage is limited to countries with complete data.

2. PQC coverage remains uneven globally.

3. Source datasets may represent different reference years.

4. Composite indicators simplify complex realities.

5. Rankings should complement—not replace—expert judgment.

---

Responsible Use Statement

QSSI is intended to inform evidence-based decision-making.

Results should be interpreted:

- cautiously
- transparently
- contextually
- comparatively

The framework should be used as a decision-support tool rather than a deterministic policy prescription.
