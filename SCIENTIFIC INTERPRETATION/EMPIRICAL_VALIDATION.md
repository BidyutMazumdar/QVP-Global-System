Abstract

This document presents the empirical validation layer of the Quantum Socioeconomic Stability Index (QSSI) framework family.

The validation architecture integrates multiple international-scale datasets and applies statistical robustness procedures to evaluate:

- structural coherence,
- ranking stability,
- perturbation resilience,
- latent dimensional consistency,
- and reproducibility potential.

The empirical framework incorporates:

- AI capability indicators,
- governance quality metrics,
- legal-institutional stability measures,
- resilience indicators,
- and multidimensional composite structures.

The results demonstrate exceptionally strong statistical consistency across the majority of framework layers, with high rank preservation under perturbation testing and strong latent structural concentration under Principal Component Analysis (PCA).

The empirical findings support the interpretation that the broader SCI/QSSI architecture exhibits substantial methodological robustness suitable for advanced comparative analytical applications.

---

1. Introduction

Composite socioeconomic frameworks frequently encounter methodological criticism related to:

- instability,
- overfitting,
- weighting fragility,
- redundancy,
- poor reproducibility,
- and low perturbation resistance.

To address these concerns, the SCI/QSSI framework family was subjected to a dedicated empirical validation pipeline integrating:

- real international datasets,
- perturbation analysis,
- correlation diagnostics,
- Monte Carlo simulations,
- PCA analysis,
- and ranking stability assessment.

The objective of this empirical layer is to determine whether the framework maintains structural consistency under computational stress conditions.

6. Correlation Interpretation

The SCI, SCI_PLUS, and LEGAL_WGI systems exhibit exceptionally strong monotonic consistency.

The AI_INDEX framework demonstrates near-perfect structural coherence across indicators.

The comparatively lower correlations observed within RES_INDEX and SCI_ULTRA suggest:

- broader multidimensional diversity,
- reduced redundancy,
