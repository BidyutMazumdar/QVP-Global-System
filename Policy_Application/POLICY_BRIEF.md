# Interpretation Guidance

QSSI is a comparative benchmarking framework.

Higher scores generally indicate stronger performance
across the evaluated dimensions.

QSSI should not be interpreted as a prediction,
forecast, or deterministic measure of future outcomes.

---

# Important Limitations

QSSI does not directly measure:

- Military capability
- GDP performance
- Political ideology
- Electoral outcomes
- Future economic growth

The framework should be interpreted alongside
national statistics, expert assessments,
and domain-specific evidence.
