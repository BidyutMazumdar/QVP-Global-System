# Executive Overview

The Quantum-Veil Sovereignty Security Index (QSSI™) provides a multidimensional analytical framework for evaluating sovereign capability preparedness across Artificial Intelligence capability, Governance and Legal Quality, National Resilience Capacity, and Post-Quantum Cybersecurity Readiness.

The framework integrates heterogeneous international datasets into a harmonized, statistically validated, reproducible, audit-traceable, and DOI-archived analytical architecture suitable for comparative sovereign assessment, policy benchmarking, institutional analysis, strategic planning, governance evaluation, resilience assessment, and capability monitoring.

QSSI™ is designed to support evidence-based policy analysis while maintaining methodological transparency, computational reproducibility, provenance traceability, and audit-grade verification standards.

# Scope Limitations

The framework does not directly measure:

- Gross Domestic Product
- Military Power
- National Wealth
- Electoral Outcomes
- Political Ideology
- Cultural Influence
- Geopolitical Influence
- Political Legitimacy

Interpretation should remain strictly within the sovereign capability dimensions represented by the underlying indicators and methodology.
