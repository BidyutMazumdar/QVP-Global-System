# 1. PURPOSE AND GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE

This Licensing Framework establishes the authoritative intellectual-property, research-distribution, third-party-rights, archival, and commercial-rights architecture governing the **QSSI™ / QVP™ Global System V11**.

The framework intentionally follows a **rights-separated, multi-layer licensing architecture** rather than treating an entire repository, publication package, dataset collection, software ecosystem, or archival bundle as subject to one blanket license.

### Governing Principle

> **Public research transparency does not constitute a transfer of commercial, operational, enterprise, implementation, or proprietary rights.**

Accordingly:

- expressly designated public research materials may be distributed under **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**;
- separately identified proprietary intellectual property remains reserved;
- commercial, enterprise, SaaS, operational, production, and implementation rights remain reserved unless separately authorized;
- third-party materials remain governed by their original licenses and applicable legal restrictions;
- no repository, publication, archive, DOI, or public disclosure creates an implied commercial license; and
- only rights that the relevant rights holder is legally authorized to license may be granted.

This architecture is designed to support:

- international academic research;
- scholarly citation;
- institutional review;
- reproducibility-oriented validation;
- archival preservation;
- methodological transparency;
- intellectual-property protection;
- third-party license compliance;
- commercial-rights reservation;
- enterprise licensing;
- controlled technology transfer; and
- future authorized technology deployment.

# 33. COMPLIANCE AND INTERPRETATION PRINCIPLE

This Framework is intended to provide a clear, auditable, and rights-separated governance model for the QSSI™ / QVP™ Global System V11.

It must be interpreted conservatively.

Where an artifact contains mixed rights, the more restrictive applicable rights regime should not be disregarded merely because the artifact is distributed within a public research package.

Where licensing status is uncertain:

1. identify the artifact and rights holder;
2. identify all applicable licenses and contractual restrictions;
3. identify third-party material;
4. verify the authority to license;
5. identify commercial and modification restrictions;
6. document the applicable rights classification;
7. obtain separate authorization where required; and
8. avoid representing uncertain material as universally licensed.

### Fundamental Governance Rule

> **No public research declaration can grant rights that the rights holder does not possess or cannot lawfully grant.**
