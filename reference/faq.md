# 🌐 General Framework

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 1. What is QSSI 2026?</strong></summary>

The **Quantum Sovereign Stability Index (QSSI) 2026** is a multidimensional sovereign benchmarking framework for evaluating institutional preparedness across:

- 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
- ⚖️ Legal Governance
- 🔐 Post-Quantum Cybersecurity
- 🛡️ National Resilience

The framework integrates multiple evidence domains into a transparent, reproducible and validation-oriented analytical architecture.

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 2. What is the primary objective?</strong></summary>

QSSI aims to provide a transparent and statistically validated framework for evaluating sovereign institutional preparedness in an era characterized by:

- Artificial Intelligence transformation
- Cybersecurity evolution
- Post-Quantum transition
- Digital governance
- Strategic resilience
- Institutional modernization

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 7. How are indicators normalized?</strong></summary>

QSSI applies **Min-Max Normalization**.

```text
(x − min(x)) / (max(x) − min(x))
```

This transforms all indicators onto a comparable scale.

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 8. How is the final score calculated?</strong></summary>

Hybrid weighting combines:

- 📊 Entropy
- 📈 CRITIC
- 🧮 Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

```text
Hybrid Weight =
(Entropy + CRITIC + PCA) / 3
```

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 9. Why use multiple weighting methods?</strong></summary>

Because hybrid weighting:

- reduces methodological bias
- improves robustness
- enhances stability
- supports reproducibility
- strengthens statistical validity

</details>

# 🔬 Validation

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 10. What validation procedures are included?</strong></summary>

The framework includes:

- 📈 Correlation Analysis
- 🎲 Monte Carlo Validation
- ⚖️ Sensitivity Analysis
- 🏆 Benchmark Validation
- 📊 Rank Stability
- 🧠 Shapley Attribution
- 🔍 Dominance Analysis
- 📚 Reproducibility Audits

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 11. What is Monte Carlo Validation?</strong></summary>

Monte Carlo simulations repeatedly perturb weighting structures and recompute rankings to estimate:

- Mean Rank
- Rank Variability
- Confidence Intervals
- Stability Scores
- Robustness Metrics

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 12. What is Sensitivity Analysis?</strong></summary>

Sensitivity analysis evaluates ranking changes under weight perturbations such as:

- ±5%
- ±10%
- ±15%

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 13. What is Dominance Analysis?</strong></summary>

Dominance Analysis measures the contribution of each strategic dimension to overall country performance.

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 14. What is Shapley Attribution?</strong></summary>

Shapley Attribution quantifies the marginal contribution of every framework component to the final composite score.

</details>

# 📈 Confidence & Data Quality

<details>
<summary><strong>❓ 15. What are Confidence Scores?</strong></summary>

Confidence Score reflects data completeness.

```text
Observed Indicators
────────────────────
Total Indicators
```

Higher values indicate greater observational completeness.
