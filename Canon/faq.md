Introduction

This document provides authoritative answers to frequently asked questions regarding the FAIR+D Canon™ Global Framework and its implementation within the Quantum-Veil Sovereignty Security Index (QSSI), QVP GLOBAL SYSTEM™, and related computational governance methodologies.

The objective is to support transparency, reproducibility, methodological clarity, and institutional understanding.

---

1. What is FAIR+D Canon™?

FAIR+D Canon™ is an institutional methodology and governance framework designed to promote transparent, reproducible, interoperable, traceable, and defensible computational research.

The framework extends conventional FAIR principles through the addition of a Defensible dimension.

---

2. What does FAIR+D stand for?

FAIR+D consists of five foundational principles:

- Findable
- Accessible
- Interoperable
- Reusable
- Defensible

Together, these principles establish a structured foundation for responsible computational science and evidence-based policy analytics.

---

3. Why was FAIR+D Canon™ developed?

FAIR+D Canon™ was developed to address growing demands for:

- Computational transparency
- Reproducibility
- Data governance
- Methodological accountability
- Institutional traceability
- Long-term preservation

The framework provides a structured architecture for managing analytical systems from data acquisition through publication.

---

4. What is the role of FAIR+D within QVP GLOBAL SYSTEM™?

FAIR+D Canon™ serves as the methodological governance layer of QVP GLOBAL SYSTEM™.

Within the QVP architecture, FAIR+D guides:

- Data provenance
- Dataset management
- Harmonization procedures
- Validation frameworks
- Reproducibility standards
- Publication workflows
- Computational accountability

---

5. What is QSSI?

QSSI refers to the Quantum-Veil Sovereignty Security Index.

QSSI is a multidimensional sovereign capability assessment framework designed to evaluate:

- Artificial Intelligence Capability
- Governance and Legal Quality
- National Resilience Capacity
- Post-Quantum Cybersecurity Readiness

---

6. Is FAIR+D Canon™ limited to QSSI?

No.

FAIR+D Canon™ is designed as a general-purpose methodological framework.

Potential applications include:

- Computational governance
- Public policy analytics
- Sovereign benchmarking
- Artificial intelligence governance
- Risk assessment
- Institutional evaluation
- Scientific reproducibility

---

7. Does FAIR+D Canon™ require open-source implementation?

No.

The framework does not mandate open-source distribution.

However, it encourages transparency, documentation, metadata preservation, and reproducibility whenever feasible.

---

8. What is meant by “Defensible”?

Defensible refers to the ability of a methodology, dataset, analytical procedure, or computational result to withstand technical, methodological, and institutional scrutiny.

Defensibility emphasizes:

- Explicit assumptions
- Transparent transformations
- Reproducible procedures
- Documented provenance
- Verifiable evidence

---

9. What is data provenance?

Data provenance refers to the complete lineage of data throughout its lifecycle.

This includes:

- Source acquisition
- Verification
- Standardization
- Harmonization
- Validation
- Integration
- Analysis
- Publication

Provenance enables traceability from final outputs back to original source observations.

---

10. Why is reproducibility important?

Reproducibility allows independent researchers to verify analytical results using identical inputs and documented procedures.

Reproducibility strengthens:

- Scientific credibility
- Institutional trust
- Methodological rigor
- Long-term reliability

---

11. How does FAIR+D support reproducibility?

FAIR+D promotes reproducibility through:

- Version control
- Metadata preservation
- Manifest documentation
- Provenance tracking
- Validation reporting
- DOI-based archival preservation

---

12. What is computational traceability?

Computational traceability is the ability to reconstruct every analytical step that contributed to a published result.

Traceability links:

- Inputs
- Transformations
- Intermediate outputs
- Final results

into a transparent analytical lineage.

---

13. What is country harmonization?

Country harmonization is the process of standardizing sovereign identifiers across heterogeneous datasets.

Examples include:

- United States of America → United States
- Republic of Korea → South Korea
- Czechia → Czech Republic
- China, People's Republic of → China

Harmonization improves interoperability and integration accuracy.

---

14. Does FAIR+D prescribe a specific statistical method?

No.

The framework is methodology-neutral.

Researchers may employ:

- PCA
- Entropy methods
- CRITIC methods
- Machine learning
- Econometric models
- Bayesian approaches

provided procedures remain transparent and reproducible.

---

15. What is the relationship between FAIR+D and governance analytics?

FAIR+D provides a governance architecture for computational decision-support systems.

The framework strengthens:

- Transparency
- Accountability
- Auditability
- Institutional trust
- Evidence-based evaluation

---

16. How does FAIR+D address long-term preservation?

The framework encourages permanent preservation of:

- Datasets
- Metadata
- Manifests
- Validation reports
- Computational records
- Publication artifacts

through persistent repositories and DOI-based archival systems.

---

17. Can FAIR+D be used internationally?

Yes.

FAIR+D Canon™ is designed to support international and cross-jurisdictional analytical projects.

The framework is intended to be platform-independent, institution-neutral, and globally applicable.

---

18. Does FAIR+D evaluate political systems?

No.

FAIR+D is a methodological framework.

It does not evaluate political ideologies, governments, or political legitimacy.

Its focus is analytical transparency and reproducibility.

---

19. What types of organizations may use FAIR+D?

Potential users include:

- Research institutions
- Universities
- Government agencies
- Policy laboratories
- International organizations
- Think tanks
- Data science teams
- Independent researchers

---

20. What is the primary objective of FAIR+D Canon™?

The primary objective is to establish a transparent, reproducible, traceable, interoperable, and defensible foundation for computational governance, sovereign analytics, and evidence-based institutional intelligence.
