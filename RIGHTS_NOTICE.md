1. PURPOSE AND GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE

This Rights Governance Notice establishes the authoritative intellectual-property, licensing, rights-separation, third-party-material, archival, provenance, and commercial-rights framework applicable to the QSSI™ / QVP™ Global System V11.

The framework adopts an artifact-level, rights-separated, multi-layer licensing architecture rather than treating an entire repository, publication package, dataset collection, software ecosystem, ZIP archive, or research project as automatically subject to a single blanket license.

Governing Principle

«Public research transparency does not constitute a transfer of commercial, operational, enterprise, implementation, proprietary, or other separately reserved rights.»

Accordingly:

- expressly designated public research materials may be licensed under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0;
- separately identified proprietary intellectual property remains reserved unless expressly licensed;
- commercial, enterprise, SaaS, operational, production, implementation, and deployment rights remain reserved unless separately authorized;
- third-party materials remain governed by their applicable original licenses, contractual terms, and legal restrictions;
- no repository, publication, DOI, archive, GitHub repository, Zenodo record, or public disclosure creates an implied commercial license; and
- only rights that the relevant rights holder is legally authorized to license may be granted.

This framework is intended to support:

- international academic research;
- scholarly citation;
- institutional review;
- methodological transparency;
- reproducibility-oriented research;
- archival preservation;
- provenance;
- intellectual-property protection;
- third-party license compliance;
- commercial-rights reservation;
- controlled technology transfer; and
- future authorized deployment.

This Notice is intended to provide a clear, auditable, and rights-separated governance model.

3. identify third-party material;
4. verify licensing authority;
5. identify commercial and modification restrictions;
